## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Choose the guest path during onboarding and use the full training experience offline.

### How do I restore a Cloud Save?

On the first onboarding screen, choose **Sign In** and use the same Apple or Google identity that
created the backup. Ironbit validates the cloud snapshot before replacing data on the phone.

### What if the phone and Cloud Save contain different data?

Ironbit does not silently merge or overwrite them. It shows factual summaries and asks whether to
keep this phone, restore the cloud backup, or cancel.

### Can I train without a network?

Yes. Local training, workout completion, and progress remain available offline. An optional Cloud
Save retries while the app is open after connectivity returns.

### How do I disconnect or delete Cloud Save?

Open **Profile → Settings → Data & Privacy → Cloud Save**.

- **Disconnect Cloud Save** signs out and keeps the phone usable as an offline guest.
- **Erase This Device** removes local player data but preserves the cloud backup.
- **Delete Cloud Account** requires fresh Apple or Google verification, removes the Supabase
  authentication account and remote backup, and keeps the phone as an offline guest.
- **Erase Everything** deletes the cloud account and then erases local player data.

### How do I change analytics or crash-report settings?

Open **Settings → Data & Privacy**. Usage Analytics can be turned off at any time. Crash Reports are
off by default and take effect after the next launch when enabled.
